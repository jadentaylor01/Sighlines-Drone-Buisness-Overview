Service 01

Deer & Game Recovery

Lost a downed deer in heavy cover, at dusk, or across a creek you can't cross on foot? A thermal drone can scan the area in minutes and pinpoint a heat signature the naked eye and a flashlight can't.

$250 per call-out

+ $100 bonus if the deer is found

  • Works through darkness, brush, and moderate cover
  • You get GPS coordinates or a guided walk-in to the location

When it won't work: thick fog, heavy rain, or dense tree canopy can block thermal detection, and a carcass cools to ambient temperature over time, making it harder to distinguish from the surrounding terrain. We'll tell you honestly if conditions aren't in your favor before you pay for a flight.

Service 02

Lost Pet Search

A missing dog or cat is an emergency. We can scan yards, fields, ditches, and wooded areas fast - especially valuable overnight when ground searches slow down.

$150 per search

+ $100 bonus if the pet is found

  • Covers open ground, treelines, culverts, and ditches
  • Works day or night; most effective overnight when it's cooler than a moving animal
  • We call you the moment we spot a heat signature matching your pet's size

When it won't work: a pet sheltering deep inside a building, culvert, or dense brush pile may not be visible from the air, and hot summer days reduce the heat contrast between an animal and its surroundings. We'll pair the flight with search guidance for ground-level blind spots.

Livestock Location
